## Final Student Submission Pack

Submit only:

1. GitHub repo branch
2. Engineering Investigation Report
3. 3-minute Evidence Defense Video

Founder reviewers may ask for a 5-minute live viva.

## Before Submitting

- [ ] I made at least 3 meaningful commits.
- [ ] I included runtime evidence.
- [ ] I included at least one rejected hypothesis.
- [ ] I connected each root cause to evidence.
- [ ] I verified the critical workflow after fixing.
- [ ] I completed the AI Verification section.
