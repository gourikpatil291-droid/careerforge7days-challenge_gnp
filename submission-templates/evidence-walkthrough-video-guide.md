# Day 1 Evidence Defense Video Guide

Length: 3 minutes maximum.

Do not read your full report.

Show evidence.

## Required Structure

## 0:00-0:20 - Identity

Name:
Variant ID:
One sentence: what was broken?

## 0:20-1:00 - First Failure

Show:

- failing UI or network request
- what you expected
- what actually happened

## 1:00-1:45 - Root Cause Evidence

Show:

- one browser/network clue
- one backend/config/code clue
- how they connect

## 1:45-2:30 - Fix And Verification

Show:

- commit or changed file
- successful review workflow after fix

## 2:30-3:00 - AI Verification

Answer:

- Did AI help?
- What did you verify before trusting it?
- What did AI miss or assume?

## Weak Video

Weak videos only say:

- "I fixed the bug."
- "The issue was in frontend."
- "I used ChatGPT and changed the port."

## Strong Video

Strong videos show:

- failing request
- log/config/code evidence
- exact fix
- post-fix verification
